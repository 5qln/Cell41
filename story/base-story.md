# The Formation Trail — the base story of Cell 41

**The α (the center):** *start from not knowing.* Genuine novelty and human value arise
from Not-Knowing (∞0), never from knowledge (K). The machine carries, never speaks; the
humans attest the meaning.

**Start here:** [Preface](preface.md) — what Cell 41 is, and the full glossary of the
words this story uses (the Word, the Bridge, the Plant, the Stone, the Lake, the Desk,
the Hold, the Membrane, the notation).

This is the base story — one chapter per run, from the base to today. It is append-only
(L3): every chapter ends in the question it opened, and the next chapter grows from it.
Side updates will be appended, never rewriting what is here.
