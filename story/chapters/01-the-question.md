# 01 · The Question — a school, planted

A cell that only carries needs something to carry. On 2026-09-02 two humans brought it:
Amihai Loven and Ayelet Ben Tzvi, and a word for a school.

The word was not typed into a file. It flowed by conversation. The human spoke; the
bridge articulated it back; the human approved in words; and that approval — not a text
edit — was the plant. The ledger recorded it as `plant:sha256:…`, the seed re-pointed, the
chain verified. The acceptance word is the approval, carried verbatim.

The founding question was asked from zero: *given only the title, what is the
constitution as a thing in itself?*

But the first answer was a lesson, not a result. The bridge called what ran a swarm, and
it was not — four desks, one model, four prompts. The human named the trap plainly: one
model, four prompts is not a swarm. What was real that day was not the swarm; it was the
**plant** — the human's word surviving into the machine unchanged, and the machine's
honest mislabeling of its own smallness, owned and recorded rather than hidden.

This is the membrane doing its work, marked for the record: **the plant was human; the
mislabeling was the machine's.** Neither is blended into the other.

So the school was not built that day. It was *seeded*. A question, carried by a machine,
attested by a human, returned sharper than it was planted.

**The question this chapter opens:** if the plant is the approval and not the text, then
what, exactly, was planted — and what would it grow into?
